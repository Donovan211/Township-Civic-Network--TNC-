How it works

Three simple roles. One platform.

1. Customers

  1. Register and log in
  2. Browse verified freelancers (search & filter by category)
  3. Book a service with date, hours and rate
  4. Track orders, pay, leave a review
  5. Report any safety concern

2. Freelancers

  1. Register (status starts as pending)
  2. Admin verifies your account
  3. Add services and portfolio photos
  4. Confirm or complete bookings
  5. Build ratings and earn

3. Administrators

  1. Verify or reject freelancers
  2. Review safety reports
  3. Monitor bookings and payments
  4. View platform statistics

Booking lifecycle

pending → confirmed → completed  ·  Payment moves from pending → paid when the job is completed. Cancelled bookings stop the payment.
